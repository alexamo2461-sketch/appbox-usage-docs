# App Collection Sorting

On the app home page, tap the menu button (three dots icon) in the top-right corner and select the "Sort" option to set your preferred sorting method. Currently, three sorting methods are available:

- **Sort by Name**: Arrange in alphabetical order ascending (A-Z) or descending (Z-A).
- **Sort by Creation Time**: Arrange from newest to oldest, or from oldest to newest.
- **Custom Sort**: Manually adjust the order of your collections to suit your personal preferences.

![Sort Options](/collection-management/sort-options-en.png)

## Custom Sort

Tap "Start Sorting" to begin custom sorting. Currently, only list view and card view support custom sorting. In grid view, custom sorting is not available and there is no "Start Sorting" button, but items will still be displayed in the custom sort order.
