# Sharing App Collection

AppBox allows you to share app collections as images and QR code links, making it easy to recommend apps to friends or import them to other devices.

## Share as Image

On the Collection Details page, tap the share button in the top-right corner and select "Share as Image." The current collection will be generated as a beautiful share image, including the collection name, description, and all app items.

## Share as QR Code Link

Select the "Share as Link" option, and the system will generate a QR code. Others can scan the QR code or tap the shared link to import the collection into their own AppBox.

## Importing Shared Collections

When you receive a shared collection link or image from others, you can import it by:

1. Tapping the menu button in the top-right corner on the AppBox homepage
2. Selecting "Import from QR Code or Link"
3. Scanning the QR code or pasting the link to complete the import
