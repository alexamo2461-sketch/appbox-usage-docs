# Adding and Editing App Collections

## Creating a New Collection

Think of an App Collection as a "list" or a "folder." On the Homepage, tap the **Add Collection** icon in the top-right corner to bring up the creation page. You can then enter the collection name, select a color, and add a description to create your new collection.

![Create Collection](/collection-management/create-collection-en.png)

## Editing a Collection

Long-press a collection to trigger the context menu, or tap the **Edit Collection** button within the Collection Details page to open the editor. From here, you can modify the collection's name, color, and description.

![Edit Collection](/collection-management/edit-collection-en.png)

## Collection Colors

When adding or editing a collection, you can set a custom color. This color is primarily reflected in the following areas of the Collection Details page:

- The background of the collection description.
- The "Add" button.
- The action menu icons in the top-right corner.
- The highlight accents in any App Details page accessed from that specific collection.

![Collection Color](/collection-management/collection-color.png)
