# Statistics

AppBox provides rich data statistics to help you fully understand your app collection.

## Overview

Tap the **Statistics** tab in the bottom navigation bar to view various data charts and analysis results.

::: info
Statistics is a Pro feature that requires an AppBox Pro subscription. A sample data preview is shown when not subscribed.
:::

## Key Metrics

Four core metric cards are displayed at the top of the statistics page:

| Metric | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Saved Apps** | Total unique apps (tap to view the full app list) |
| **Collections** | Total number of created collections |
| **Subscription Spending** | Total cost of all subscription apps (tap for details) |
| **Total Apps** | Total app entries without deduplication (tap to view the raw list) |

## Statistical Charts

### Rating Distribution
Shows the distribution of ratings you've given to apps, helping you understand your rating preferences.

### Subscription Data
Displays current subscription count, monthly spending by currency, estimated annual cost, and more. Tap to enter the detailed subscription data page.

### Description Coverage
Shows how many apps have descriptions and the coverage percentage, helping you identify apps without descriptions.

### Adding Trends
Monthly statistics on the number of apps you've saved, helping you understand your collecting habits.

### Platform Distribution
Shows the distribution of supported platforms (iPhone, iPad, Mac).

### Region Distribution
Shows the App Store country/region distribution of your apps.

### Category Distribution
Shows the distribution of apps by their primary App Store category.

## Statistics Scope

By default, statistics cover all collections. You can customize the scope:

1. Tap the **Scope** button in the top-right corner of the statistics page
2. Select **All Collections** or check specific collections
3. Enable **Cross-collection Deduplication** to merge duplicate apps from different collections

## Custom Statistics

You can customize the display order and visibility of statistics cards:

1. Tap the **Customize** button in the top-right corner of the statistics page
2. Drag to adjust the card display order
3. Tap the eye icon to toggle card visibility
4. Tap **Reset** to restore the default layout

## Subscription Details

Tap "Subscription Spending" or "Subscription Data" cards to enter the detailed subscription page, including:

- Monthly spending statistics
- Annual spending statistics
- Total spending trends
- Subscription breakdown
- Cost distribution charts
- Time and renewal analysis
