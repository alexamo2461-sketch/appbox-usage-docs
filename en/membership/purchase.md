# Purchase Instructions

> Related: [Pro Feature Overview](/en/membership/feature-overview)

## Purchase Notes

- As features continue to improve, the price may be adjusted. The developer promises: one-time purchase users will not be converted to subscriptions — no additional payment is needed after purchase.
- All features are available during the membership period. Refunds are not supported after purchase, so please consider carefully before buying.
- Purchases/subscriptions cannot be transferred between different Apple ID accounts. Membership cannot be shared across multiple App IDs.

## Restore Purchase

If you have already purchased the membership but cannot use its features during use or after reinstalling, go to Settings - AppBox Pro page and manually tap the "Restore Purchase" button to restore your previous membership status (you must use the same Apple ID used for the purchase). If there is no "Restore Purchase" button on the AppBox Pro page, your membership is already active.
