# Pro Feature Overview

> Related: [Purchase Instructions](/en/membership/purchase)

This product will continue to be taken seriously, and your support is my reason to keep investing time and effort.

Some advanced features of AppBox require an AppBox Pro subscription (hereinafter referred to as "membership") to use. The current membership is a one-time purchase — once purchased, you can use it permanently, including all future advanced features. Membership features include:

::: info
- **iCloud Sync**: Upload data to iCloud (requires a valid iCloud account) and enable cloud sync functionality.
- **Share App Collections**: Share app collections as images and QR code links, and import shared links/images into your own app.
- **Record App Attributes**: Support for recording subscription-related attributes (including subscription description, price, currency, subscription type, and date) as well as app price attributes.
- **Add Custom Apps**: Support for customizing app information by uploading icons, filling in app descriptions, sources, etc., as well as importing and exporting custom apps.
- **Batch Import**: Support batch recognition and import of multiple links from long text into app collections.
- **Data Statistics**: Provides rich data statistics charts, including rating distribution, subscription spending, platform distribution, region distribution, category distribution, and more.
- **App Tags**: Add custom tags to apps, with support for tag management, filtering, and sync.
- **Card View**: Add card view to app collection detail pages — display app ratings, descriptions, tags, and more through cards.
- **Data Export**: Support exporting data as JSON or ZIP format files.
- **Data Import**: Support importing data exported from AppBox as well as App Store records from Apple's Data & Privacy download.
- **App Display Configuration**: Customize the display/hide of app descriptions and tag display modes.
:::

Features marked as "In Development" are not yet available but will definitely be included in future versions.

The membership price will increase over time as new features are added (excluding special promotions). If you find the app useful, it's a good idea to purchase it early at a lower price 🥰~
