# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the AppBox iOS/iPadOS/MacOS application.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.  
All of your data created in the app (such as collections, notes, and ratings) is stored only on your device. Data will only be uploaded to iCloud if you manually enable iCloud synchronization in the app, allowing your data to sync across your devices. We do not store or process your data on any servers.

## Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs. We do not share or transfer your data with any third parties.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device or in iCloud (after enabling sync), you have full control over the security and management of your data. Please ensure proper management of your device and iCloud account to prevent data loss or leakage.
