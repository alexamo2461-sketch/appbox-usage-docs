# Add via Share Extension

::: info
📌 For systems below iOS 16, the Share Extension is not supported directly within the App Store. You can use the [Add via Shortcuts](/en/how-to-add-apps/qr-link-import) method instead.
:::

Long-press an application icon on the Home Screen to display the context menu, then tap the **Share App** button. From the share sheet that appears, select **AppBox** to open the "Add to Collection" page.

![Share Extension](/collection-management/share-extension-en.png)

## Usage Scenarios

- Open an app page in the App Store and save it with one tap.
- Share store links from Safari to AppBox.

## Steps

1. Open a page containing an App Store link in the source app, or select the link text (depending on the source app).
2. Tap **Share** (the box with an upward arrow icon).
3. In the share sheet, find **AppBox** (the name and icon will appear as listed in the system), and tap it.
4. If AppBox is not in the list: scroll to the bottom, tap **Edit Actions…** or **More**, then add AppBox to your favorites or toggle it on.
5. In the extension interface, follow the prompts to select the **Collection** you want to add to, then confirm.
6. Return to AppBox and check the corresponding collection for the new app.

## Notes

- The Share Extension works with content that the system identifies as "links" or "text." If the source app restricts sharing types, AppBox may not appear.
- Data is saved locally in the selected collection.

## Recommendation

Pin AppBox to the top of your share sheet for faster saving.

## Troubleshooting

> **Tip**: If you can't find AppBox on first use, make sure the main app is fully installed from the App Store, then toggle the switch on in **Edit Actions**.

**Q: AppBox doesn't appear in the share sheet?**
A: Go to the bottom of the share sheet and edit actions. If it still doesn't appear, restart your device and try again, or temporarily use the paste link method instead.
