# Editing App Descriptions

You can add custom ratings and notes to apps in a collection. The ratings and notes will be displayed below the app icon and title in the app info section.

**How to access**: There are multiple entry points:

1. Tap the edit button in the top-left corner on the app details page
2. The app preview area after adding an app via the bottom toolbar's add module
3. The app preview area after recognizing an app via the add button on the collection details page

![Editing App Descriptions](/app-management/edit-description.png)

> **Note**: Notes for the same app name across different collections are independent. For example, if "TikTok" exists in both Collection A and Collection B, their ratings, notes, and subscription attributes are independent and will not sync. This is a feature, not a bug. To make the notes identical, use the copy feature to manually copy the app to another collection. After that, editing the notes in one collection will not sync to the other.
