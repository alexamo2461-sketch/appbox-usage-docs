# Copying/Moving Apps

On the collection details page, long-press an app and tap the "Copy/Move" button in the pop-up menu to copy or move the app to another collection. You can select multiple target collections when copying, but only one when moving.

You can also use multi-select to copy/move multiple apps at once: [Multi-Select Operations](/en/app-management/multi-select)
