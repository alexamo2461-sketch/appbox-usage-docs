# Share Management

The Share Management page is used to manage your shared collection links.

**How to access**: Settings → Collections → Share Management

## Overview

After generating share links through the "Share Collection" feature, you can manage all active share links here in one place.

## Management Operations

### View Share Links

The list displays all currently active share links, including the collection name and share status.

### Disable a Single Share

Tap a share link to disable that specific share. Once disabled, others will no longer be able to import the collection through that link.

### Disable All Shares

Disable all active share links with one tap.

### Auto-disable on Collection Deletion

When this option is enabled, deleting a collection will automatically disable its associated share links, preventing invalid links from remaining active.

## Share Options

Configure what content is included when sharing:

- **Include app descriptions**: Include each app's custom description when sharing
- **Include collection description**: Include the collection description text when sharing
- **Include subscription info**: Include subscription-related attributes when sharing
- **Include app tags**: Include app tag information when sharing

::: info
Share Management is a Pro feature and requires an AppBox Pro subscription.
:::
